TECHNOLOGY · PROFILE

Local Engineer Caught
Building Software At All Hours

Electrical engineering undergraduate refuses to pick a side, ships full-stack platforms and neural networks anyway — neighbours report keyboard noise well past midnight

By OUR TECHNOLOGY CORRESPONDENT · Photographs by the front-facing camera

BANGALORE, SEPT 18 — Soorya S Joshi, an Electrical & Electronics Engineering undergraduate at REVA University, has been working steadily across a border most people respect: the one between hardware and software.

Sources close to the matter — chiefly a GitHub contribution graph — confirm production-minded code in Java, C++, Python and TypeScript, along with the systems built around it: APIs, data models, interfaces and the analysis that proves any of it works.

The body of work is unusually wide for a third-year student. It includes a deployed platform that detects abandoned websites, a PyTorch super-resolution model trained end to end without external APIs, a learning platform designed for short attention spans, and embedded fault-detection hardware built on Arduino.

Mr Joshi currently analyses process data for the Process Engineering team at Cepheid, a Danaher company, in Bangalore. He has also served internships at Bharat Electronics Limited and Adani Dahanu Thermal Power Station, and co-authored an IEEE publication on the kinematic analysis of a 20-degree-of-freedom humanoid robot.

Asked to explain the range, associates point to a simple through-line: two disciplines, one toolchain. Neither the circuit nor the compiler is treated as somebody else's department.

INVESTIGATION

Abandoned Websites: The Silent Epidemic Nobody Is Scanning For

One platform reads DNS, HTTP, TLS, server fingerprints and content metadata, then scores the evidence — and shows its working

SITE SCOUT — A full-stack platform, built and deployed, that analyses websites for the signs of abandonment or ongoing maintenance.

Its explainable 0–100 abandonment score is assembled from stale Last-Modified and copyright dates, outdated dependencies, legacy HTML, placeholder content and missing security headers. No single signal convicts; the report names every one it used.

Under the surface: asynchronous scanning, technology fingerprinting, historical scan storage, trend analysis, and CSV/JSON reporting — shipped with both a CLI and a web interface over the same engine.

ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

Man Teaches Computer To See Four Times Better, Locally

NEURALENHANCE · PYTORCH

An end-to-end 4× super-resolution system that trains and infers entirely on local hardware — no external AI APIs anywhere in the loop.

A residual convolutional architecture with PixelShuffle upsampling reconstructs high-frequency detail; a combined pixel and Sobel-gradient loss keeps edges sharp rather than smoothed. Quality measured in PSNR and SSIM, with a Gradio interface for upload, before/after comparison and export.

Robot Walks In Twenty Different Ways; Paper Confirms Each One

Mathematical modelling and kinematic analysis of a 20-DOF humanoid, co-authored and published

IEEE PUBLICATION · CO-AUTHOR

The work applies mathematical and computational methods to model joint coordination and analyse robot kinematics across a full articulated chain of twenty degrees of freedom.

WORD OF THE DAY

heisenbug (noun)

A defect that alters its behaviour the moment you attempt to observe it. Vanishes under the debugger; returns the instant you tell your manager it is fixed. See also: works on my machine.

Used in a sentence: “It is not a heisenbug, it is a race condition, and it is mine.”
